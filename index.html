Student Tracking, Without the Guesswork

Students rarely cancel without warning signs. A missed check-in, a drop in engagement, a couple of skipped weeks. The signs are there, but without a system tracking them, they're easy to miss until it's too late.

The Hub turns every weekly and monthly check-in into a living timeline per student: weight evolution, training frequency against their own goal, and mood on a simple scale, all in one card. Instead of scrolling through WhatsApp history to remember how someone's doing, you open their profile and see the full story.

The feature that matters most here: "View Churn Risk Only." One click filters your entire student base down to the people who need your attention this week, not after they've already cancelled.

Your Business Health, in One View

Running training as a business means answering questions most trainers only guess at. Is revenue actually growing? Which students are worth the most over time? Where are new students really coming from?

The BI tab puts your MRR, active students, average ticket, renewal rate, churn rate, and LTV on a single screen, updated automatically, not recalculated by hand once a month. Below that, an audience profile reads your own check-in and sales data back to you: dominant gender, top age range, main goal, preferred plan, primary channel, peak activity hours. It's the kind of segmentation agencies charge for, built in.

And as your data grows, so does the system: the built-in ML Forecast model activates automatically once you have enough history, projecting where your numbers are headed instead of just where they've been.

From Lead to Payment, in One Place

A lead that lives in your DMs is a lead you'll eventually lose track of. The Sales tab exists to make that impossible.

Register a new lead, pick their plan, and generate a payment link on the spot, no separate checkout tool, no manual invoice. Every lead then moves through a visual pipeline (New → Contact → Proposal → Closed → Lost), so you always know exactly how many people are one conversation away from becoming a paying student, and how many slipped through last month.

No more sales happening in your head.

Revenue & Margin Optimization

THE CHALLENGE

48,620 transactions revealed a critical imbalance: 59.5% of annual revenue was generated between 12PM and 5PM, while the evening/closing shift consumed proportional labor and inventory for only 8.1% of total revenue, a direct drag on operating margin.

THE STRATEGY

Data-driven shift reallocation: 50% of workforce and stock redirected to the 12PM–5PM peak window. KPI deployment aligned logistics to real-time demand patterns, exposing Friday as the highest-yield day (+37% vs Sunday) and Large pizzas as the top revenue driver at 45.9% of total sales.
